# Study Dentistry in Europe in English

Desire2Study helps international students from the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada, the Middle East, and other regions **study dentistry in Europe** — with full support and access to accredited, English-taught dental schools.

### 🦷 Why Study Dentistry in Europe?

- No HPAT, UCAS or UK GAMSAT restrictions  
- Fully accredited dental degrees taught in English  
- Recognised across Europe, the UK, US, Canada, and beyond  
- Affordable tuition fees from €13,500  
- Direct entry after high school — no pre-dental required
